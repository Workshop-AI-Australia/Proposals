03 · How we decide what to buildFour questions before any agent.

Not every job should be an agent. Before we build one, it has to pass four questions. A clear "no" on any of the first three means we don't build it. The fourth decides how much it's allowed to do on its own, which is how your judgement stays protected.

1

Is the input structured?

Can the work start from something consistent, like your quote form or a plan set, not a vague one-line email?

2

Are the steps definable?

Can we write down what it does, the same way each time? Your pricing is largely fixed formulas, so much of it can.

3

Is the output checkable?

Can you look at the result and tell quickly whether it's right? With the workings shown, you can.

4

What's the cost of a wrong answer?

Low and reversible: the agent acts. High or hard to undo: the agent drafts, you commit.

This is why a price never sends on its own. The agent does the legwork; you stay on the trigger.
