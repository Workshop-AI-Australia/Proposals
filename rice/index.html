03 · How we decide what to buildFour questions before any agent.

Not everything should be an agent. Before we build one, the job has to pass four questions. A clear "no" on any of the first three means we don't build it as an agent. The fourth sets how much it's allowed to do on its own.

1

Is the input structured?

Can the work start from something consistent, like an invoice or a report, not a vague request?

2

Are the steps definable?

Can we write down what it does, step by step, the same way each time?

3

Is the output checkable?

Can a person look at the result and tell quickly whether it's right?

4

What's the cost of a wrong answer?

Low and reversible: the agent acts. High or hard to undo: the agent suggests, a person commits.

This is why your financial work is built to suggest, not act. The agent does the heavy lifting; a person stays on the trigger.
